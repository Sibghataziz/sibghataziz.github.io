Khabar Capsule – खबर कैप्सूल

Short Hindi news videos, every day.

What it is

Khabar Capsule is a Hindi news channel on YouTube and Instagram. Each video is a 30–45 second animated summary of one news story, narrated in Hindi, with on-screen captions.

How the app works

The "khabar-capsule-youtube" app is an automated publishing tool used only by the channel owner. It prepares the videos and uploads them to the Khabar Capsule YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API (permission: youtube.upload). It does not sign in other users and does not read or store anyone else's Google data.
