Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy describes how the Khabar Capsule app ("khabar-capsule-youtube") handles information.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by the owner of the Khabar Capsule channel to publish videos to that channel. It is not offered to the public, and no other people sign in to it.

Google user data

  • The app requests one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, used only to upload videos and set their title, description and tags on the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • The app does not read, collect, store, share or sell any other Google user data.
  • The OAuth refresh token is stored privately as an encrypted secret in the owner's GitHub repository and is used only by the owner's automated upload job.

Viewers

People who watch Khabar Capsule videos on YouTube or Instagram interact with those platforms directly; their own privacy policies apply. This app does not collect any information about viewers.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.
